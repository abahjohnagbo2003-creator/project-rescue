WELCOME

A safe place to begin again.

Project Rescue is built around faith, accountability, community and a practical path toward freedom from addiction. Whether you are struggling with alcohol, drugs, dishonesty, or another destructive habit, the first step can simply be asking for help.

We believe a person's worst chapter does not have to be the end of their story. Our goal is to encourage spiritual growth while connecting people with appropriate recovery and professional support when it is needed.

⭐ MEMBER OF THE WEEK

Sandra Giggs

“I see a new purpose.”

Sandra describes growing up in a difficult home where she felt responsible for caring for her younger siblings. She says she learned to lie to survive, and eventually alcohol became part of her life too. Today, she is choosing honesty, recovery and a different future.

Her reminder: Your past can explain your pain without deciding your future.
